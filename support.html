FAQ

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Open Scribbloom, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. Family members sharing a purchase can restore the same way.

How does iCloud sync work?

Turn on Sync with your iCloud in Scribbloom's Settings (it's off until you turn it on). Your notebooks, notes, handwriting, transcripts and study cards sync through your own private iCloud. Audio recordings and imported PDF files stay on the device where you made or added them and don't sync, so make sure iCloud is on for Scribbloom in the Settings app, and that you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device. We can't see your synced data.

Which languages can be transcribed?

Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition, so it supports the languages your device can transcribe offline. On iOS 26 and later, the transcript appears live while you record, and if your language needs a one-time speech model from Apple, Scribbloom downloads it the first time you transcribe. On iOS 17 to 18, the transcript is made after you stop recording, which takes longer for long recordings. Transcripts made after you stop are free; live transcripts while you record are part of Scribbloom Pro.

Do I need permission to record a class or meeting?

Often, yes. Some states and countries require everyone being recorded to agree, and many schools and workplaces have their own rules. Ask first and let people know. This isn't legal advice.

How do I export notes, PDFs or study cards?

For an imported PDF, open it, tap More (…) and choose Export Flattened PDF (your ink and highlights drawn in) or Export with Editable Annotations. For study cards, open a deck, tap More (…) and choose Export CSV, or use Export All as CSV in the Cards tab's + menu. The CSV has front, back and deck columns, so you can open it in a spreadsheet or import it into another flashcard app (Scribbloom can import the same format). Typed and handwritten notes don't have an export option yet.

Why don't I see AI summaries or study card suggestions?

Summaries, AI study cards, Quiz Me and Ask Scribbloom are part of Scribbloom Pro, and they need a device that supports Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later) with Apple Intelligence turned on. Everything else works on any supported device, and you can always make study cards by hand.

System requirements

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 17 or later
  • AI summaries and study card suggestions need an Apple Intelligence-capable device with Apple Intelligence turned on
  • Apple Pencil is optional; you can draw with your finger
