Privacy Policy

Scribbloom · Effective October 11, 2026

The short version

Data Not Collected

Scribbloom doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no third-party tracking or advertising tools are inside the app. We don't run any servers for it to talk to.

What stays on your device

Your notes, handwriting, imported PDFs, recordings, transcripts, summaries and study cards are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. We can't see them, and they aren't sent to us or anyone else.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on sync (it's off by default), your notebooks, notes, handwriting, transcripts and study cards are stored in your own private iCloud database, under your Apple Account. Audio recordings and imported PDF files are never synced; they stay on the device where you made or added them. Apple handles that storage under its own privacy policy. As the developer, we have no access to your private iCloud data. Turning sync off keeps everything on the device only.

AI and transcription happen on your device

Transcripts, summaries and study cards are made by Apple's on-device speech and Apple Intelligence models. Your audio and text are never sent to us, to Apple's cloud AI, or to any other service for this.

Permissions, and why

  • Microphone: only to record audio when you start a recording.
  • Speech recognition: to turn your recordings into text, on your device.
  • Notifications (optional): local reminders when study cards are due. They are scheduled on your device and you can turn them off any time.

You can change any of these in the Settings app. Scribbloom still works without them, minus that feature.

Purchases

The Pro unlock is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we don't receive your payment details. Your purchase is checked on your device.

Children's privacy

Scribbloom collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions? Email jvappsupport+scribbloom@proton.me.
