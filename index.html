Scribble it. Let it bloom.

Notes for iPhone and iPad that grow into something you remember. Write, mark up PDFs and record, and Scribbloom helps turn it all into transcripts, summaries and study cards, right on your device.

Data Not Collected

What it does

  • Handwriting and PDF markup
    Write with Apple Pencil on iPad or your finger on iPhone, and mark up lecture slides and PDFs side by side with your notes.
  • Recordings synced to your ink
    Record a class or meeting, then tap anything you wrote to hear that moment again.
  • On-device transcripts and summaries
    Live transcripts while you record (when your device supports on-device speech) and short summaries are made on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Study cards that come back at the right time
    Turn your notes into study cards and review them with spaced repetition.
  • Data Not Collected
    No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Optional sync uses your own iCloud.

Free to download with unlimited notes. One optional $3.99 Pro unlock, paid once, works on both iPhone and iPad and includes Family Sharing.
